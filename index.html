Pocket Skies

You are the weather.

A tiny island sits on your desk. Pinch the sun to swing its shadows, drag clouds to make it rain, and bloom every plant. Hands only, no controllers.

To play in mixed reality, open hexa3.github.io/pocket-skies in the Meta Quest Browser. You can also try it right here with a mouse.
